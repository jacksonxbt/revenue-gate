# Revenue Gate | Product specification

Status: concept selected, runtime validation pending. BNB Bangkok submission: 27 Nov 2026, 09:00 ICT.

## Product
Product first, speculation second. A reusable OpenFour launch experience connecting observable product payments with launch milestones. Flagship: one read-only AI financial research service. Hub preferred; Boost fallback depends on partner credentials. Testnet only.

## MVP journey
Discover product → request research → pay with testnet funds → receive sourced report → view payment evidence and milestone → inspect actual OpenFour launch state. Distinguish informational milestones from contract-enforced eligibility. Test payments are not commercial revenue or proof of independent customers.

## Scope
One product, one payment flow, one milestone, one meaningful OpenFour integration. Three views: discovery, research workspace, launch dashboard. No mainnet, real funds, autonomous trading, buybacks, custody, multi-chain or complex vesting.

## Acceptance
Working integration, reproducible setup, actual testnet evidence, clear limitations, 2–3 minute demo and public repository before submission. Developer confirms architecture and actual runtime results.
